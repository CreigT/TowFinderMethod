CHP freeway tow: stored or impounded?

California Highway Patrol separates towed vehicles into two main categories: stored vehicles and impounded vehicles.

Stored vehicle

CHP says a stored vehicle can generally be retrieved directly from the tow company by the registered owner or agent. Examples include collision-damaged vehicles, vehicles left on a freeway too long, or vehicles creating a traffic hazard.

Impounded vehicle

An impounded vehicle requires a release from the CHP office that caused the impound. CHP states that the tow company will not release an impounded vehicle until directed by CHP.

What to have ready

  • License plate or VIN
  • Registered-owner information
  • Government-issued identification
  • Any registration or release documents requested by the handling CHP office

If you do not know where it went

CHP advises contacting the office that caused the tow if you are unsure why the vehicle was towed or where it was taken.

Storage-fee warning

Confirm whether the vehicle is stored or impounded, which tow company has it, whether a CHP release is required, and the current charges before traveling.
