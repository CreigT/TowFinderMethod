Riverside County towed vehicle release

For certain Sheriff-ordered tows, the registered owner may need a Vehicle Release Form before going to the storage yard.

Official path

Riverside County Sheriff — Vehicle Release

The Sheriff’s FAQ says the registered owner may need to go to a Sheriff’s Office front counter to obtain a Vehicle Release Form for vehicles towed for reasons such as parking violations, expired registration, or safekeeping.

Open official Riverside Sheriff source ↗

Required documents

  • Valid driver’s license, or a person with you who has one
  • Current vehicle registration
  • If the registered owner cannot appear, a notarized authorization may be required

Storage-fee warning

Confirm whether a Sheriff release is required, which storage facility has the vehicle, the current charges, and pickup requirements before going to the yard.
