San Bernardino County Sheriff — Tow Service Program

The Sheriff’s Department uses approved tow operators for law-enforcement towing. The handling station or agency is the best starting point when you need to identify where the vehicle was stored or what release step applies.

Open official Sheriff tow source ↗

What to have ready

  • License plate or VIN
  • Registered-owner information
  • Government-issued identification
  • Proof of ownership or registration

Storage and property

The Sheriff’s Tow Service Agreement requires participating tow operators to maintain approved storage facilities and release personal property under applicable conditions. Exact vehicle-release requirements depend on the tow reason and handling agency.

Storage-fee warning

Do not assume the first tow yard you find online has the vehicle. Confirm the handling station, tow operator, current charges, release requirements, and pickup hours first.
