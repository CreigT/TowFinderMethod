LAPD impound: use the official release path.

If LAPD impounded your vehicle, the official LAPD guidance directs owners to the Area Auto Detective Unit responsible for the impound.

Official path

Los Angeles Police Department — Vehicle Impound

For a 30-day hold, LAPD says the registered owner generally must wait until the hold ends unless an authorized early-release exception applies. Vehicle release and impound-hearing questions are handled through the geographical Area Auto Detective Unit.

Open official LAPD source ↗

What to have ready

  • License plate or VIN
  • Proof that you are the registered owner or otherwise entitled to possession
  • Valid California driver’s license, or a licensed driver with you when required
  • Current registration and insurance when applicable to your release

Important timing

LAPD states that impound-hearing requests must be made to the station that impounded the vehicle within 10 days of the date on the written notice. Auto detectives are listed as available during regular business hours.

Storage-fee warning

Impound and storage charges can increase while the vehicle remains in custody. Confirm the exact release requirements, garage location, and current total with the official LAPD/Official Police Garage source before traveling or paying.
